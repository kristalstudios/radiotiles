Support

Questions, ideas or a station that won't play? Write to support@radiotiles.com. We read every message.

How do I add a station?

Open Explore on the home screen, search by name or pick a country and genre, and tap +. To add a station by its stream link, go to Settings › Add Station and paste the link.

A station doesn't play

Stations run their own streams, and sometimes a stream moves or goes offline. Try another link for the same station in Explore, or add a second stream link in the station's settings, one per line. Radio Tiles switches to the next link if the first one doesn't respond.

How do I make it play when I get in the car?

In Radio Tiles, open Settings › CarPlay autoplay and follow the short steps. They set up a Shortcuts automation that plays the radio as soon as your iPhone connects to CarPlay or your car's Bluetooth.

What's free and what's in Pro?

Free: up to 3 stations, CarPlay, Apple Watch, widgets, Siri and the weather. Radio Tiles Pro adds unlimited stations, every artwork style and the sleep timer. Pro is available monthly, yearly with a 7-day free trial, or as a one-time lifetime purchase.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings › Radio Tiles Pro › Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro. Family Sharing is supported.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Radio Tiles Pro. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid renewal. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.
